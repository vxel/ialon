User-interface font of Ialon
============================

font-jackofgears.fnt / font-jackofgears.png are a bitmap rendering (BMFont format) of :

    Jack of Gears Regular, version 1.0
    Designed by Michael W. Moss
    https://michaelwmoss.com  -  mechanismatic@gmail.com

Licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The typeface covers printable ASCII only, so the accented letters used by French are synthesized
from its own glyphs (base letter + a squashed/sheared bar, caret or dot pair as the diacritic).

The bitmap page and its .fnt descriptor are derivative works of that typeface, generated from
core/src/test/resources/fonts/jack-of-gears/Jack of Gears Regular.ttf by the BitmapFontGenerator
tool of this project (./gradlew :core:generateFont). They are redistributed under the same
CC BY 4.0 terms; the rest of Ialon is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0
(see COPYING).
